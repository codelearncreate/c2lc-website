# Say Bonjour to French Language Support on Weavly!

{{< img src="images/weavly-french.png" alt="A screenshot of Weavly in French language." >}}

We're excited to announce a major new feature that brings Weavly to French users: full French language support is now live!

If you're a French speaker or would like to use Weavly in French, our application now offers a seamless, fully localized French experience from the interface you see to the messages your screen reader speaks.

## What's Included

### User Interface Translations
Every button, label, heading, navigation menu, and announcement has been translated into French with accuracy and context in mind. The new French mode provides a smooth, intuitive experience tailored to French users.

### Accessible Screen Reader Messaging
We've ensured that all content, including image descriptions, and dynamic announcements used by screen readers, which are often used by blind people and people with low vision, are also translated into French. This ensures an inclusive experience for all users, regardless of how they interact with Weavly.

### Seamless Language Switching
Users can switch between English and French at any time using the new language selector at the top left corner. Your language preference is remembered across sessions, so your experience is always just the way you want it.
